Min Ruei is a first-year PhD student in the [Human Medical Genetics and Genomics Program (HMGGP)](https://www.cuanschutz.edu/graduate-programs/human-medical-genetics-and-genomics/home)
at the University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus, currently rotating with Dr. [Milton Pividori](https://pivlab.org/members/milton-pividori)
and Dr. [Cheryl Ackert-Bicknell](https://www.cuanschutz.edu/graduate-programs/human-medical-genetics-and-genomics/faculty/Ackert-Bicknell-Cheryl-UCD6002641597).

She received her M.S. in Nutritional Sciences from Cornell University. Her long-term research interest is in using computational genomics to predict individual differences in metabolism and inform precision nutrition.
