Spencer is a first-year PhD student in the [Human Medical Genetics and Genomics Program (HMGGP)](https://www.cuanschutz.edu/graduate-programs/human-medical-genetics-and-genomics/home) at the University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus, currently rotating with Dr. [Milton Pividori](https://pivlab.org/preview/pr-64/members/milton-pividori.html).

He graduated from UCLA with a B.S. in Molecular, Cell, and Developmental Biology. His current research interests include computational genomics, bioinformatics, and methods development for characterizing complex disease phenotypes.
